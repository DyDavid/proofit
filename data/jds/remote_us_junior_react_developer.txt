ABOUT NORTHWIND LABS
Northwind Labs builds observability tooling for platform engineering teams. We are 42 people, fully distributed across the United States, and we have been remote-first since we were founded in 2019. We write our decisions down, we review asynchronously, and we keep meetings to two fixed blocks a week.

ABOUT THE ROLE
You will join the Product Frontend team of five engineers and own features in our dashboard from ticket to production. This is a junior title but it is not a training role: we expect you to have shipped React to real users already and to be comfortable owning a feature end to end with review support.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Own dashboard features from ticket through design review, implementation, testing and release
- Profile and fix rendering performance problems in data-heavy React views
- Extend our component library and its Storybook documentation
- Take part in the weekly release rotation, including a production smoke check
- Write the customer-facing release note for the features you ship

REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
- 2+ years of professional experience building and shipping production React applications
- Expert-level React, including hooks, context and performance profiling
- Strong TypeScript skills
- Experience consuming and designing REST and GraphQL APIs
- Experience with automated testing using Jest, React Testing Library and Cypress
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and deploying to AWS or Vercel
- Demonstrated ownership of features in an Agile team, from ticket to production
- Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship
- Excellent written English for an asynchronous, remote-first team

NICE TO HAVE
- Familiarity with Next.js or Remix
- Experience with state management libraries such as Redux or Zustand
- Open-source contributions or a public portfolio
- Comfortable with at least 4 hours of daily overlap with US Pacific time

WORK AUTHORIZATION
Northwind Labs is unable to provide visa sponsorship or employ candidates located outside the United States.

HOW TO APPLY
