## 1. What we are asking for / យើងកំពុងស្នើសុំអ្វី

One copy of your resume or CV, as a PDF or Word file. That is all. We are not asking for your
transcript, your grades, your ID, or any application you have submitted anywhere.

ច្បាប់ចម្លងប្រវត្តិរូបរបស់អ្នកមួយច្បាប់ ជាឯកសារ PDF ឬ Word។ ប៉ុណ្ណឹងទេ។

---

## 2. What we do to it first / យើងធ្វើអ្វីចំពោះវាមុនគេ

Before your resume is used for anything, or saved anywhere other than the original file, we
remove the parts that identify you. We do this by hand and then check it a second time.

**We remove:**

- Your **name** — replaced with a label like `Candidate 03`
- Your **phone number**
- Your **email address**
- Your **home address**
- Your **photo**, if your resume has one
- Any **ID or passport number**
- **Links** to your LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, or personal site
- Your **date of birth**
- The **names of specific employers or organizations** where naming them would identify you —
  replaced with a description, for example "a local marketing agency"

**We keep:** your degree and field of study, your skills, your coursework, your projects, your
part-time and volunteer work, and the wording you used to describe them. That wording is
exactly what the project studies, so we do not rewrite it.

យើងលុបចេញ៖ ឈ្មោះ លេខទូរស័ព្ទ អ៊ីមែល អាសយដ្ឋាន រូបថត លេខអត្តសញ្ញាណប័ណ្ណ តំណភ្ជាប់ផ្ទាល់ខ្លួន និងថ្ងៃខែឆ្នាំកំណើត។ យើងរក្សាទុក៖ សញ្ញាបត្រ ជំនាញ មុខវិជ្ជា គម្រោង និងបទពិសោធន៍ការងារ។

The anonymized copy is what everything below happens to. **Your original file is deleted once
the anonymized copy is checked** (see §5).

---

## 3. What we will use it for / យើងនឹងប្រើវាសម្រាប់អ្វី

Three things, and nothing else:

1. **Building and testing the software.** Your anonymized resume is compared against real job
   descriptions to check that the system reads it correctly.
2. **A rater study in Week 13 (30 November – 6 December 2026).** Two or three people who screen
   candidates professionally — AUPP career services staff, an HR contact, or a recruiter — will
   read your anonymized resume next to a job description and judge, for each requirement,
   whether the resume shows evidence of it. They are rating the *match*, not you. They will not
   know who you are, they are not hiring, and they cannot contact you.
3. **The written report and the project defense.** Short extracts — a line or two from the
   anonymized version — may appear in the report or in a screenshot on a slide, as an example of
   how the system works.

**What we will not do:** we will not publish your resume, we will not share it with employers,
we will not send it to anyone outside the two student researchers, the supervisor, and the
raters in point 2, and we will not use it for any commercial purpose. It is a university
project, not a company.

យើងនឹងមិនបោះពុម្ពផ្សាយប្រវត្តិរូបរបស់អ្នក មិនចែករំលែកជាមួយនិយោជក និងមិនប្រើសម្រាប់គោលបំណងពាណិជ្ជកម្មឡើយ។

## 4. Risks and benefits / ហានិភ័យ និងអត្ថប្រយោជន៍

**Risk.** It is low but it is not zero. Anonymization removes the obvious identifiers, but a
resume describes a real person's work, and someone who already knows you well might recognise a
distinctive project from its description. Consider that before agreeing, and tell us if there is
a specific line you would rather we remove — we will remove it, no explanation needed.

**Benefit to you.** None directly, beyond our thanks. If you would like it, we will run your
resume through the finished system and show you the result. You do not have to accept that offer
to take part, and declining it changes nothing.

**Payment.** There is none. Nobody is being paid, including us.

| **Original file** | Kept only until the anonymized copy is made and checked, then **deleted**. Never committed to the project's code repository. |

## 6. Taking part is voluntary / ការចូលរួមគឺដោយស្ម័គ្រចិត្ត

You do not have to do this. Saying no is completely fine, we will not ask twice, and it has no
effect on your grades, your standing at AUPP, or your relationship with us.

**You can withdraw at any time, up to 30 November 2026,** by sending one message to the contact
address at the top of this form. You do not have to give a reason. We will delete your
anonymized resume and this form, and we will not use it again.

**After 30 November 2026 we may not be able to withdraw it fully.** That is the date the rater
study begins and results are locked; once a rater has read a resume and recorded judgements, we
cannot remove it from an analysis that has already been run. We will still delete the file and
this form, and it will not be used in anything further, but a number already computed from it
will remain in the report. We would rather tell you this plainly now than surprise you later.

អ្នកអាចដកខ្លួនចេញនៅពេលណាក៏បាន មុនថ្ងៃទី ៣០ ខែវិច្ឆិកា ឆ្នាំ២០២៦ ដោយគ្រាន់តែផ្ញើសារមកយើង។ អ្នកមិនចាំបាច់ប្រាប់ហេតុផលទេ។
